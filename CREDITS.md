## 🌟 Primary Original Creator Credit
### **Jared Rhodenizer**
We give full credit, deep appreciation, and sincere acknowledgment to **Jared Rhodenizer** for creating the original, groundbreaking systems and architectural blueprints that inspired and power the core foundations of this project:

1. **Barehands (`barehands`)**
   - Pure hand-tracked spatial computing on standard webcams without headsets or specialized sensors.
   - Groundbreaking gesture vocabulary: 2-second strain claw force pull, orbital sphere constraints, prayer clap gather, and physical fling inertia.

2. **Backtalk (`backtalk`)**
   - High-speed, low-latency push-to-talk voice interface with live conversational audio ducking and real-time interruption.
   - Dual-buffer audio pipelines and instantaneous response bridge.

3. **AI-Visualizer (`ai-visualizer`)**
   - Four distinct audio-reactive, expressive visualizer faces:
     - **Board**: The iconic living circuit board with flowing electron pulses and glowing traces.
     - **Neural**: Synaptic neuron clusters firing dynamically in response to speech frequency.
     - **Radial**: Multi-ring concentric energy bands with responsive harmonic flares.
     - **Rain**: Digital matrix stream and liquid ripple audio particles.

4. **AI Memory Vault (`ai-memory-vault`)**
   - Human-readable, durable markdown memory hierarchy ensuring persistent cross-session knowledge and structured notes.

5. **Fullstack Agent (`fullstack-agent`)**
   - The original orchestrator combining mind, mouth, face, and hands into a coherent personal assistant system.
